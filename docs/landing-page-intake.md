## Single-message question batch

Before I start the landing page, please answer these together. Short answers are fine; write “use your judgment” for anything you want me to decide.

1. **Primary action:** What is the one action visitors should take: open the editor, download the app, start a trial, book a demo, buy, or join a waitlist?
2. **Offer:** Exactly what will visitors get, including access, features, pricing or trial details?
3. **Conversion:** What counts as success: a click, signup, download, purchase, or another event?
4. **Audience:** Who is the ideal customer or user?
5. **Problem:** What main problem are they trying to solve?
6. **Objections:** What are the top three reasons they might hesitate or leave?
7. **Traffic:** Where will visitors come from: search, ads, social media, email, or somewhere else?
8. **Prior knowledge:** What will visitors already know about the product when they arrive?
9. **Proof:** What real logos, testimonials, measured results, customer examples or case studies can we use?
10. **Product assets:** Which screenshots, demo videos or product GIFs are available, and may I create captures from the working editor?
11. **Terms and risk reversal:** What guarantees, refund terms, cancellation terms, free access or trial conditions can we truthfully state?
12. **Voice:** Should the writing sound casual, professional, or something more specific?
13. **Design direction:** Which visual direction do you want, such as minimal editorial, playful illustration, glass UI, or a reference of your choice?
14. **Mobile priority:** How important is the mobile experience compared with desktop, and are there any device requirements?

## Required design output after intake

Before landing page code, the skill requires this order:

1. Page outline and section order.
2. Hero headline, subheadline, CTA and proof line.
3. Three to five outcome-focused benefits.
4. Three-step explanation of how it works.
5. Six to twelve FAQ questions with answers.
6. Index/noindex recommendation and, if indexed, page title and meta description.
7. Layout recommendation A, B, C or D with a reason.

Then build section by section according to A6. Preserve the source's mandatory tagline reveal section and accessibility, states and reduced-motion requirements. For real marketing use, proof must be real or clearly labeled as illustrative; the source's sample-content suggestions do not establish actual customers, testimonials or performance figures.
